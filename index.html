A LETTER FOR YOUR BIRTHDAY

Dear Polly,

Happy birthday! ♡

I wanted to give you something a little different for your special day. Something you could read whenever you wanted, and maybe smile at even after today has passed.

I'm glad I got the chance to come back to you. Even though I've done something to you, you're someone I'm genuinely happy to be with.

I remembered that pink is your favorite color, so I wanted to fill this little corner of the internet with pink skies. And of course, I couldn't leave out your sunflowers. 🌻

I hope this new chapter of your life brings you beautiful memories, peaceful days, new adventures, and plenty of reasons to smile. I hope you get to enjoy the little things, chase what makes you happy, and feel appreciated for the person you are.

There's one more thing I wanted to tell you. Something simple, but something I mean.

✦

ONE LITTLE FACT

I love you with all my heart.

Not a promise about tomorrow. Not an expectation for you to say it back. Just an honest feeling I wanted you to know about.

I still remember you don't like promises. I'd rather be honest about what I feel and let my actions speak for themselves.

You don't have to find the perfect words for this letter. I just hope it makes your birthday a little more special.

Keep being yourself, keep finding reasons to smile, and never forget that your birthday is worth celebrating.

Happy birthday once again. 🌻

With sincerity,

Your Lucky Clover Vari ♡
